📜 Community Rules

To keep TheRoPlayer Tools safe, secure, and useful for all Roblox developers, everyone must follow these core platform rules.

1. Uncopylocked Assets Only

All uploaded games (.rbxl) and scripts (.lua) must genuinely be uncopylocked or open-source community tools. Do not upload stolen copyrighted paid plugins or malicious backdoored assets.

2. Clean & Respectful Interaction

Harassment, hate speech, spamming comments, or posting malicious links in comment sections or user bios will result in an immediate and permanent account ban.

3. No Malicious Scripts / Viruses

Uploading scripts containing intentional grabbers, webhooks stealers, or trojans will result in an immediate blacklist and ban from the platform.

4. Consequences of Breaking Rules

Administrators hold full authority to review, edit, or delete violating posts and ban users. Banned users are completely blocked from entering the website until an administrator manually unbans their account.
